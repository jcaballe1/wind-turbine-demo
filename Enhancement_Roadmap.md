
### Why this specific prompt works:

*   **Font Pairing:** Right now, your app uses one master font (Inter). Humans designing custom apps almost always pair a serif (for authority/tradition) with a sans-serif (for readability).
*   **Hard Shadows (Neubrutalism):** AI defaults to soft, blurry shadows. Hard, solid drop shadows instantly communicate a modern, bespoke graphic design trend that looks intentionally crafted.
*   **Color Specificity:** AI loves Tailwind's default color wheel. By forcing custom hex codes (like a "paper" background and an "ink" text), the app immediately separates itself from 90% of standard React templates.
*   **Tone of Voice:** AI tends to write UI copy like: *"Error: Material cannot exceed 500kg."* A human writes: *"Whoa there! An offshore turbine would rip itself apart with that much material. Let's scale it back under 500kg."* Humanizing the microcopy is the ultimate anti-AI measure.
